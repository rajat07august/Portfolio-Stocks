## 1. Business in Plain English

**Gelatin** is a protein extracted from animal bones and hides — used to make **pharmaceutical capsule shells** (every hard-shell capsule you swallow), **gummy candies**, **desserts** (jelly, marshmallows), **premium food thickeners**, and **collagen peptide supplements** (the wellness/nutrition category exploding globally). **Nitta Gelatin India** is the Indian arm of the Japan-based **Nitta Gelatin Inc.** — a 100+ year-old Japanese specialty ingredients company. This is a **50-year-old joint venture between the Kerala family (Kapadia/Vijayan lineage) + Nitta Gelatin Japan**, producing gelatin, collagen peptide, and ossein (the bone-derived intermediate) from a manufacturing base in Kochi, Kerala.

The 30-second business snapshot:
- **HQ Kochi, Kerala** — founded 1975 (50-year heritage)
- **Ownership**: Nitta Gelatin Inc. (Japan parent) + Indian promoter families
- **FY26 estimated revenue**: ~INR 600 cr; FY26E PAT ~INR 90-100 cr

## 2. Why This Industry Exists

Gelatin + collagen peptide + ossein rides five durable forces:

**Force 1 — Global pharmaceutical capsule shell demand.** Every hard-shell capsule you swallow contains gelatin. India + China are largest capsule shell producers. Growing 5-7% CAGR globally.

**Force 2 — Wellness + nutrition megatrend for collagen peptide.** Global collagen peptide market projected to grow from $600M (2024) to $2B+ (2030). Beauty + joint health + protein supplement categories. Nitta Gelatin Japan is a global technology leader here.

**Force 3 — India as low-cost raw material source.** India has abundant bovine + porcine bone availability (byproduct of leather + food industry) — feedstock advantage.

**Force 4 — Nutraceutical + food premiumisation.** Premium gummy candies + wellness drinks + protein bars all use collagen peptide + gelatin.

**Force 5 — Regulatory + halal + kosher requirements.** Indian producers with multi-certification are preferred for regulated markets.

**Where Nitta sits.** #1 Indian specialty gelatin + collagen peptide player with Japanese-parent technology. Peers: **Sterling Biotech** (in insolvency), **Pioma Industries**, **India Gelatine & Chemicals**. Nitta's positioning: **Japanese-JV technology + collagen peptide leadership + Kerala manufacturing scale**.
