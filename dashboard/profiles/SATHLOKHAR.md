## 1. Business in Plain English

When a pharma company wants a new factory, a warehouse operator needs a distribution centre, or a data centre developer wants a full-scale campus built, they need an **integrated EPC (Engineering, Procurement, Construction) contractor** that can handle everything — architecture + civil works + Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB) + Mechanical/Electrical/Plumbing (MEP) + solar + interior fit-outs — all in-house. **Sathlokhar Synergys is a Chennai-based integrated EPC contractor** targeting industrial + warehousing + healthcare + pharmaceutical + food processing + renewable energy sectors.

The 30-second business snapshot:
- **HQ Chennai, Tamil Nadu** — founded 2013 by G. Thiyagu (Promoter, Chairman, MD, CEO — one-person management)
- **NSE-SME listed** — small size, only 5,655 shareholders
- **FY26 revenue crossed INR 800 cr milestone** — record year

- **Business model**: 30-35% civil labor + majority engineering-driven (unusual for EPC)
- **9-year marketing investment**: INR 7 cr+ generating 60,000+ leads, 23,717 prospects, 29+ industrial expos

**Big picture in one sentence:** Sathlokhar is a **fast-growing integrated EPC contractor** transitioning from labor-intensive construction to engineering-driven execution, with FY26 revenue crossing INR 800 cr, Q4 EBITDA doubling, backward integration into PEB manufacturing coming online Aug 2026, and Class 1A PWD Registration unlocking large infrastructure projects. **High-growth SME thesis — with SME liquidity constraints and one-person management risk.**

## 2. Why This Industry Exists

Integrated EPC in India rides five durable forces:

**Force 1 — India's industrial capex cycle.** Pharma + electronics + food processing + warehousing all building new facilities.

**Force 2 — Warehousing + logistics boom.** E-commerce + 3PL + cold chain driving warehouse construction demand.

**Force 3 — Data centre buildout.** Google, AWS, Microsoft, MEITY all pushing India data centre capacity.

**Force 4 — Renewable energy execution.** Solar + wind installations need EPC partners.

**Force 5 — Engineering-driven vs labor-intensive shift.** Automation + PEB + modular construction reduce labor dependency.

**Where Sathlokhar sits.** Small mid-cap integrated EPC. Peers: Larsen & Toubro (much larger), Ceigall India (recently IPO'd), NCC, Ircon, GR Infraprojects. Sathlokhar's niche: mid-value industrial + institutional buildings with in-house integrated capability.
