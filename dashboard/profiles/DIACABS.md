## 1. Business in Plain English

Diamond Power Infrastructure Ltd (brand: **DICABS**) is a **55-year-old Vadodara-based integrated power T&D solutions company** — manufacturing conductors, LV/HT/MV/EHV cables (up to 400 KV), transmission towers, and offering EPC services. Once a ₹2,674 cr revenue business (FY14) that collapsed into insolvency (FY21/FY22 near-zero revenue), the company was **acquired by GSEC Group through NCLT/CIRP in 2022** and rebuilt under new promoters. Today it's the fastest-compounding stock in the Power T&D space with **Q3FY26 PAT +692% YoY and 9M FY26 revenue ₹1,214 cr (+55% YoY)**.

**The 30-second business snapshot:**
- **Founded 1970**; conductor manufacturing pioneer; brand DICABS
- **India's largest single-location cable manufacturing setup** (Vadodara-Savli Phase II) — 6 MVC lines pre-2026, **7th line commissioned May 2026**, **3 more lines expected by 31-Mar-2027**

- **Products serve**: State DISCOMs (11 named in Q3FY26 PPT: GETCO, GSECL, JVVNL, JdVVNL, MPMKVVCL, MGVCL, MSEDCL, MSETCL, PGVCL, UGVCL, WBSEB), Central PSUs (POWERGRID, SAIL, BHEL, AMNS), Corporates (Adani group — Solar/Wilmar/Infra/Renewables, JSW Renewables, ACME Solar, WAAREE, KP Group, Prozeal, CleanMax, ABB India, Ambuja, Serum Institute)

## 2. Why This Industry Exists

Power T&D component demand is India's most durable capex vector:

1. **India's power capacity trajectory** — from ~460 GW installed (2024) targeting 900+ GW by 2032. Every GW of generation needs 3-5x the T&D capex. India historically underinvested in T&D.
2. **RDSS (Revamped Distribution Sector Scheme)** — ₹3.03 lakh cr Central + State scheme through FY26 mandating loss reduction + smart metering + underground cabling.
3. **Renewable evacuation** — 500+ GW RE target 2030 requires massive HV/EHV cable + conductor + tower network from Rajasthan/Gujarat solar zones to load centers.
4. **Import substitution + Make in India** — imported cables face 20%+ duty; PLI schemes favor domestic manufacturing. DIACABS with 55-yr manufacturing base + 550 KV capability is a preferred domestic vendor.
5. **UHV corridor + reconductoring wave** — HVDC + 765 KV substation expansion by PGCIL. **ECHO Conductor technology** (Q3FY26 breakthrough) allows 3x transmission capacity on existing towers WITHOUT retrofit — 30-40% lower reconductoring cost vs traditional.
6. **National Building Code (NBC) push** — RETAIL LV cable market opening up per new building code mandates. Q3FY26 mgmt investing @ 3,000 kms/month of retail-oriented LV cables per NBC compliance.
7. **CREDAI real estate partnership** — Q3FY26 named DICABS as National Cable Partner for FY26; ~30 million housing units in India's real estate pipeline are cable-hungry.

DIACABS is directly aligned with all seven forces — the delivery risk is turnaround execution, not demand.
