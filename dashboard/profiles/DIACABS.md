## 1. Business in Plain English

Diamond Power Infrastructure Ltd (brand: **DICABS**) is a **55-year-old Vadodara-based integrated power T&D solutions company** — manufacturing conductors, LV/HT/MV/EHV cables, transmission towers, and offering EPC services. Once a ₹2,674 cr revenue business (FY14) that collapsed into insolvency (FY21/FY22 near-zero revenue), the company was **acquired by GSEC Group through NCLT/CIRP in 2022** and re-launched under new promoters. Today it's rebuilt to ₹1,564 cr TTM revenue (Dec 2025) with expansion in progress.

**The 30-second business snapshot:**
- **Founded 1970**; conductor manufacturing pioneer in India
- **India's largest single-location cable manufacturing setup** (per Investor PPT FY24): 6 MVC lines pre-2026, 7th line commissioned May 2026, 3 more expected by March 2027
- **Capacity**: 2,50,000 MTPA conductors (ACSR, AL, AL59, new-gen); 19,500 KM MVC cables + 2,000 KM EHV cables per annum; UHV cables up to **550 KV**
- **India's largest HV R&D laboratory** (proprietary)
- **6.3 MW captive wind energy at Kutch** (being revived per FY24 PPT)
- **Products serve**: State DISCOMS, EPC contractors, industrial power projects, private utilities, exports (16 Indian states + international)
- **New promoter (from 2022)**: **GSEC Ltd** (Gujarat-based infra/financial services group), took control through NCLT resolution plan
- **Ranked 6th largest Electrical Equipment company in India** by market cap (per FY24 PPT — as of May 2024; ranking may have moved further up given 104% stock price gain since)

## 2. Why This Industry Exists

Power T&D component demand is India's most durable capex vector:

1. **India's power capacity trajectory** — from ~460 GW installed (2024) targeting 900+ GW by 2032 requires massive T&D buildout. Every GW added needs 3-5x the T&D capex. India lags T&D vs generation historically.
2. **RDSS (Revamped Distribution Sector Scheme)** — ₹3.03 lakh cr Central + State scheme through FY26 mandating loss reduction + smart metering + underground cabling. Cable + conductor demand structurally elevated.
3. **Renewable evacuation** — 500+ GW RE target 2030 requires massive HV/EHV cable + conductor + tower network to move power from Rajasthan/Gujarat solar zones to load centers.
4. **Import substitution + Make in India** — imported cables/conductors face 20%+ duty; PLI schemes favor domestic manufacturing. DIACABS with 55-yr manufacturing base + 550 KV capability is a preferred domestic vendor.
5. **UHV corridor buildout** — HVDC + 765 KV substation network expansion by PGCIL requires specialty conductors + tower solutions.

DIACABS is directly aligned with all five forces — but the delivery risk is the turnaround execution, not demand.

## 3. What Makes This Company Different (Moat)

Six moats + honest weaknesses, grounded in FY24 PPT + Screener data:

1. **India's largest single-location cable manufacturing setup** (FY24 PPT verbatim). Vertical integration + scale economics + logistics advantages. 7th MV/EHV line commissioned May 2026, 3 more by March 2027 — adds ~450 KM/month capacity.
2. **UHV cable capability up to 550 KV** — small handful of Indian companies (Sterlite Power, KEC, Apar Industries, DIACABS) have this. Specialty entry barrier.
3. **India's largest High-Voltage R&D laboratory** (per FY24 PPT). Barrier for testing + certification for HV/EHV grades. Competitors have to send products elsewhere for validation.
4. **55-year legacy manufacturing know-how** — set up cable manufacturing in 1970, LV cables 1995, UHV lab 2008, UHV cables 2011. Institutional knowledge + trained workforce.
5. **Post-CIRP capital structure clean-up** — massive debt haircut through NCLT resolution; new promoter GSEC brought fresh capital; borrowings ₹367 cr (FY23) down from ₹1,977 cr (FY22) — deleveraged balance sheet ready for growth.
6. **Multi-product integrated model** — Conductors + LV/HT/MV/EHV Cables + Transmission Towers + EPC + Wind Energy — one-stop for utilities + EPC contractors. Unique in India.

**Where the moat is weaker (7 real risks):**

- **Negative book value: ₹-13.6/share** (reserves ₹-770 cr Sep 2025). Historic distress hasn't fully cleaned up — technically the equity has negative carrying value. Any earnings-multiple valuation must acknowledge this.
- **Legal overhang recently discharged but not fully clean.** 8-May-2026 disclosure: *"Company discharged in CBI/ED/PMLA matters; assets worth 1,000+ Cr and receivables 900+ Cr may be released."* — this is a positive but reveals the severity of the pre-2022 issues.
- **MPS non-compliance for Q4FY25.** BSE + NSE fined ₹4.5 lakh each (July 2026 disclosure). Minimum public shareholding requirements missed — governance friction point.
- **Concall transcript absent.** Screener only lists PPTs, no Q&A transcripts. Reduces analyst-question coverage. Contrast with well-run mid-caps that hold full transcript concalls.
- **Promoter over-concentration (84% Mar 2026).** Down from 95% peak (Mar 2024) but still above SEBI's 75% cap for continuing MPS compliance. Further dilution likely required.
- **Massive earnings volatility.** FY14 profit ₹104 cr → FY17 loss ₹-795 cr → FY22-23 losses → FY24 turned positive ₹17 cr → TTM ₹98 cr. Historic pattern of extremes.
- **No formal Q&A discipline — no analyst call → less external accountability** vs peers YASH + SYSTEMATIC (both do concalls).

## 6. Why the Market Is Paying This Multiple

### The valuation puzzle

**P/E 105x on FY26 TTM ₹98 cr PAT looks expensive at face value.** But:
- Post-CIRP companies routinely trade at premium multiples because "book value" is uninformative after debt haircuts
- Q3FY26 run-rate implies ₹180-200 cr forward PAT (0.87 EPS × 4 = ₹3.48; × 52.7 cr shares = ~₹184 cr)
- On forward, P/E drops to ~55-60x
- Market is pricing in continued sequential quarter-over-quarter momentum + CBI/ED discharge unlock

### Bull / Base / Bear (2-year horizon)

**Base case (50% probability):** Revenue grows 40% FY27 to ₹2,200 cr (vs TTM ₹1,564 cr); OPM steady at 10-11%; PAT ~₹200 cr FY27, ₹280 cr FY28. Multiple derates modestly to 40x → mcap ~₹11,200 cr = **+9% from ₹10,321 cr**.

**Bull case (25% probability):** CBI/ED asset unlock (~₹1,900 cr) + 4 new cable lines fully commissioned + demand tailwind → FY27 revenue ₹2,600 cr, FY28 ₹3,300 cr, FY28 PAT ~₹400 cr. Multiple sticks at 45x → mcap ~₹18,000 cr = **+74%**.

**Bear case (25% probability):** CBI/ED discharge appealed + MPS forced dilution at ₹150-160 + capacity ramp slower than expected → FY28 PAT ~₹150 cr, multiple compresses to 30x → mcap ~₹4,500 cr = **-56%**.

**Weighted 2-year return:**
- Base 50% × +9% = +4.5%
- Bull 25% × +74% = +18.5%
- Bear 25% × -56% = -14.0%
- **Net: ~+9% over 2 years = ~4.5% CAGR + 0% dividend**

**Frank read:** valuation is stretched relative to fundamentals; upside depends on continuation of extraordinary sequential growth + asset unlock. **This is a high-variance turnaround bet, not a compounder** — bear case is real (-56% is not a black swan). Speculative allocation only.

### Margin of Safety

Thin. Post-CIRP companies with negative book value + no dividend + 105x P/E + MPS non-compliance don't have traditional MoS. The MoS is entirely in the operational momentum — if the quarter-over-quarter compounding of PAT continues, valuation looks fine; if it flattens, downside is severe.
