## 1. Business in Plain English

Every time India lays new water pipeline — whether for the Jal Jeevan Mission (bringing tap water to 190 million rural households), city sewage systems, urban water supply, or industrial process water — someone has to manufacture the **Ductile Iron (DI) pipes** that carry the water. These are large-diameter (100mm-2000mm), high-pressure, corrosion-resistant pipes that last 100+ years. **Electrosteel Castings (ECL) is India's largest independent manufacturer of DI pipes** — supplying to state water boards, municipalities, urban local bodies, and export markets across Middle East, Africa, and Southeast Asia.

## 2. Why This Industry Exists

Water infrastructure rides five durable forces:

**Force 1 — Jal Jeevan Mission 2.0 (INR 8.69 lakh cr through Dec 2028).** Central Govt spending INR 3.59 lakh cr (up 73% from earlier INR 2.08 lakh cr allocation). Multi-year, multi-crore demand pipeline.

**Force 2 — River linking project.** Government's much-anticipated inter-basin water transfer program — massive DI pipe demand when executed.

**Force 3 — Sewage + urban water networks.** AMRUT + Swachh Bharat + Smart Cities all driving urban water infrastructure demand.

**Force 4 — Irrigation modernisation.** Ministry of Jal Shakti driving pressurised irrigation systems.
