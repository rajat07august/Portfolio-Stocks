## 2. Why This Industry Exists

Laminated tube packaging rides five durable forces:

**Force 1 — Global personal care premiumisation.** Every consumer product from face cream to hand sanitiser now needs premium tube packaging vs traditional bottles.

**Force 2 — Beauty & Cosmetics category explosion.** Global beauty market growing 8-10% CAGR; premium tubes are the packaging of choice.

**Force 3 — Sustainability mandate.** Aluminum tubes replaced by plastic laminates → now moving to fully recyclable laminate structures. EPL leads with sustainable formats (38% of sales).

**Force 4 — Emerging markets FMCG expansion.** India + Brazil + Southeast Asia + Latin America growing rapidly.

**Force 5 — Oral care universal demand.** Every person on the planet uses toothpaste tubes → durable base demand.

**Where EPL sits.** #1 globally. Peers: Amcor, Sonoco, Berry Global (larger diversified packaging). **EPL is the specialised global leader in laminated tubes** — no direct pure-play listed peer at scale.
