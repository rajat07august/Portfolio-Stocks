## 1. Business in Plain English

Every tube of toothpaste, face cream, hand sanitiser, hair colour, and cosmetic gel you buy comes in a **laminated tube** — a multi-layered plastic + aluminum barrier construction that keeps the contents fresh, non-corrosive, and premium-looking. **EPL Limited (formerly Essel Propack) is the world's #1 laminated tube manufacturer** — 30%+ global market share, present across 11 countries with 20+ manufacturing plants. Customers include Colgate, Procter & Gamble, Unilever, L'Oréal, Estée Lauder, and hundreds of other global FMCG brands.

The 30-second business snapshot:
- **HQ Mumbai** — founded 1982 (43-year heritage)
- **Owner (till 2020)**: Ashok Goel family (Essel Group); **Blackstone acquired majority in 2020**; **Blackstone recently reduced stake** from 51% to 26% via OFS
- **MD & Global CEO**: Mr. Hemant Bakshi (Unilever alumnus)
- **COO**: M.R. Ramasamy
- **CFO**: Deepak Goyal
- **FY26 revenue growth: +13% YoY**; Q4FY26 growth **+17.6%** (highest ever in 5 years)
- **FY26 EBITDA margin: 20.4%** (7 consecutive quarters of 20%+ margin)
- **FY26 PAT growth: +15%**
- **Beauty & Cosmetics: +30% YoY FY26** (star performer, now larger than Oral Care in most key markets)
- **Oral Care: +10% YoY**
- **All 4 regions delivered double-digit growth**: EAP +25%, Americas +24.1%, Europe +15.5%, AMESA +10.4%, India +11.5%
- **Net Debt/EBITDA: 0.52x** (deleveraging)
- **Personal Care & Beyond mix: 53%** (diversification from Oral Care)
- **Sustainable tube formats: 38% of sales**
- **EcoVadis Platinum ESG rating** (top 1% globally — only Indian packaging company)

**Landmark event Q4FY26:** **Proposed merger with Indovida announced** — creates nearly **$1B consumer packaging platform** with broader portfolio + margin-accretive.

**Big picture in one sentence:** EPL is the **world's #1 laminated tube maker** in an **accelerating growth phase** — FY26 revenue +13%, Beauty & Cosmetics +30%, 20%+ EBITDA margin sustained, Blackstone stake reduced (51% → 26%) creating cleaner ownership, and **transformational Indovida merger announced creating $1B platform**. **Trading at 18.5x P/E on 17.8% ROCE + 2.11% dividend yield — best value + quality combo in packaging.**

## 2. Why This Industry Exists

Laminated tube packaging rides five durable forces:

**Force 1 — Global personal care premiumisation.** Every consumer product from face cream to hand sanitiser now needs premium tube packaging vs traditional bottles.

**Force 2 — Beauty & Cosmetics category explosion.** Global beauty market growing 8-10% CAGR; premium tubes are the packaging of choice.

**Force 3 — Sustainability mandate.** Aluminum tubes replaced by plastic laminates → now moving to fully recyclable laminate structures. EPL leads with sustainable formats (38% of sales).

**Force 4 — Emerging markets FMCG expansion.** India + Brazil + Southeast Asia + Latin America growing rapidly.

**Force 5 — Oral care universal demand.** Every person on the planet uses toothpaste tubes → durable base demand.

**Where EPL sits.** #1 globally. Peers: Amcor, Sonoco, Berry Global (larger diversified packaging). **EPL is the specialised global leader in laminated tubes** — no direct pure-play listed peer at scale.
