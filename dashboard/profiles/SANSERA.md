## 1. Business in Plain English

Where Happy Forgings makes precision forged components (crankshafts, con rods) for CV + tractor + industrial, **Sansera Engineering is India's most vertically-integrated precision components company** — spanning ICE auto components (petrol/diesel engine parts) + xEV (electric vehicle motor components + battery casings) + **Aerospace, Defence, Semiconductor (ADS)** — the transformational bet that's driving the FY26 story.

The 30-second business snapshot:
- **HQ Bangalore, Karnataka** — founded 1981, listed 2021
- **FY26 revenue INR 3,498 cr** — highest ever
- **FY26 EBITDA margin ~17%** (improving quarter-over-quarter)
- **ADS revenue INR 315.5 cr (+155% YoY!)** — the strategic bet
- **Q4FY26 revenue INR 998.7 cr** (highest ever quarterly)
- **Pantnagar new facility** inaugurated FY26
- **Nichidai Corporation (Japan) JV** signed FY26 — precision components for Japanese OEMs
- **Multiple divisions**: Auto ICE (largest), xEV (EV agnostic + specific), **ADS (Aerospace/Defence/Semiconductor)** — growth engine
- **Global manufacturing**: India + US + Sweden

**Big picture in one sentence:** Sansera is transitioning from an **auto-only precision components player** to a **multi-industry precision engineering platform** with ADS as the growth engine — riding auto capex + EV transition + India Semi Mission 2.0 + defence indigenisation simultaneously. Priced at 60.4x P/E — expensive but reflects the ADS narrative.

## 2. Why This Industry Exists

Sansera plays across four verticals:

**Force 1 — Domestic auto capex cycle.** Indian OEMs are undertaking large-scale capex — new platforms, new engines, new EV models. Precision components suppliers benefit.
**Force 2 — 2W EV acceleration.** India 2W EV market growing rapidly. Sansera has "xEV and tech-agnostic offerings" that serve both ICE and EV.
**Force 3 — Global aerospace TAM.** Boeing + Airbus + Embraer + defence platforms all need precision machined components. India = strategic manufacturing hub.
**Force 4 — India Semiconductor Mission 2.0.** Semi capex up-cycle globally driven by AI. Precision components for semi equipment.

**Where Sansera sits.** It's a **larger + more diversified** version of Happy Forgings — trades higher margin auto for wider diversification across auto + non-auto.
