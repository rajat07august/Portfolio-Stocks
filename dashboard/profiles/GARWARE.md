### ⚠️ The headline first, then the correction v1 needs

**Q1FY27 is, on the numbers, the best quarter in Garware's history — and it is a real beat, not an accounting one.** Revenue ₹633cr (+28% YoY, +6% QoQ), PAT ₹133cr (+60% YoY). There is no tax credit, no exceptional item, no other-income rescue driving the profit line. Operating profit rose ₹110cr → ₹172cr YoY, a 56% increase on 28% revenue growth — that is operating leverage doing exactly what management said it would.

**But the company's own headline — "EBITDA Margin Crosses 30%" — needs one honest adjustment.** GHFL's EBITDA definition **includes other income**. Q1FY27: operating profit ₹172cr + other income ₹20cr = ₹192cr, ÷ ₹633cr = 30.3%. Strip the other income (which is treasury yield on a ₹774cr cash pile, not film-making) and the **clean operating margin is 27.2%**. Cross-checked: the same arithmetic reconciles Q1FY26 (₹110cr + ₹13cr = ₹123cr ✓) and Q4FY26 (₹135cr + ₹21cr = ₹156cr ≈ ₹157cr ✓), so this is the company's consistent convention, not a one-quarter presentation choice.

**27.2% is still an all-time record** — the prior best operating margin in the 13-quarter series was 25% (Jun-2024). *This distinction is my adjustment, not management's stated position.* I flag it because a reader comparing "30.3%" to management's guided **25% ± 2%** band would conclude the beat is 330bps when on a like-for-like operating basis it is ~220bps. Both are beats. One is bigger than it is.

**On the anti-dumping item — the distinction matters and the press release blurs it.** GHFL says the DGTR recommendation "is expected to enhance GHFL's competitive advantage." A **DGTR recommendation is not a duty.** It goes to the Ministry of Finance, which must issue a customs notification before anything is collected, and MoF has historically declined DGTR recommendations. The scope also matters: the recommendation as described covers **TPU-based PPF from China** — that is the PPF segment (~25–31% of revenue), not the Sun Control Film business that is 50% of revenue. **Treat this as a probable-but-unbanked tailwind to one segment, and check the gazette before crediting it.** *(This framing is mine; management has presented it as a straightforward positive.)*

**⚠️ The honest counterweight to the margin beat:** management guides 23–27% and delivered 27.2% operating. That is the *top* of its own band, not a new plateau — and the deck's own medium-term box still says **"EBITDA Margin 23–27%"** for the forward period. **Management is not guiding 30% to hold.** Anyone extrapolating Q1's margin into a FY28 model is extrapolating past what the company itself is willing to say. *My inference, not management's stated position: the Q1 print benefited from a soft Q1FY26 comparator (the tariff year) plus favourable realisations, and some mean-reversion toward 25% is the base case.*

## 1. Business in Plain English

Garware Hi-Tech Films makes **specialty polyester films** for premium applications — not commodity films, but **highly engineered, multi-layered coated films** for demanding uses: (a) **Paint Protection Films (PPF)** — the clear self-healing polyurethane film applied over car paint to prevent scratches (Tesla, Porsche, Mercedes, BMW users know it), (b) **Solar/Window Control Films** — the tinted films on car windows and buildings for heat rejection, (c) **Industrial Films** — for solar panels, safety glass, and specialty industrial applications. Where the polyester film industry is largely commodity, Garware plays at the **premium value-added end** with proprietary formulations and coatings.

The 30-second business snapshot:
- **HQ Mumbai (Vile Parle)** + Plant in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad)
- **Founded 1957** — 68-year Garware family heritage
- **Director Sales & Marketing**: Deepak Joshi
- **CFO**: Abhishek Agarwal
- **Q4FY26 EBITDA INR 157 cr (+29% YoY, margin 26.2%)** — highest ever quarterly profitability
- **Q4FY26 PAT INR 108 cr (+39.1% YoY)**
- **FY26 was tariff-challenged but concluded strong**
- **Key products**: PPF (paint protection films), Sun Control Films (SCF), Solar Control Films, Industrial Films
- **Export-heavy**: Major markets US, Europe, Middle East, Asia
- **Q3FY26 saw most tariff impact**; recovered strongly in Q4

**Big picture in one sentence:** Garware Hi-Tech Films is India's **premier specialty polyester film manufacturer** — trading at 47.2x P/E on 18.0% ROCE + 26.2% Q4FY26 EBITDA margin (record) + 39.1% PAT growth. **Global specialty film player with premium PPF + Sun Control + Industrial mix.**

## 2. Why This Industry Exists

Specialty polyester films rides four durable forces:

**Force 1 — Premium car ownership growth.** Every luxury + premium car sold now increasingly needs PPF protection (globally $2-4B market). Growing 15-20% CAGR.

**Force 2 — Building energy efficiency mandates.** Window films for solar control mandated in green buildings + urban premium construction.

**Force 3 — Solar panel + safety glass.** Industrial films embedded in solar panels + safety glazing.

**Force 4 — Global shift to premium coated films.** Away from commodity BOPET → to value-added coated + laminated films.

**Where Garware sits.** #1 Indian specialty film maker in premium categories. Peers: **Uflex** (commodity + specialty, larger), **Jindal Poly Films** (largest BOPET, commodity), **Cosmo First** (specialty). Garware positioning: premium-only specialty coated films for global brands.
