## 1. Business in Plain English

Every corporate bond, bank loan, or debt instrument in India needs a **credit rating** — an independent third-party opinion on the borrower's likelihood of default. Bankers won't lend without one; institutional investors won't buy without one; regulators (RBI, SEBI, IRDAI) require them. **CARE Ratings (branded as CareEdge)** is India's **#2 credit rating agency** (behind CRISIL, ahead of ICRA + India Ratings) — with additional adjacent businesses in ESG ratings, advisory, analytics, AI-credit platforms, and international ratings (Africa + Nepal + IFSC).

The 30-second business snapshot:
- **HQ Mumbai** — founded 1993 (33-year heritage as credit rating agency)
- **MD & Group CEO**: Mehul Pandya
- **FY26 consolidated revenue INR 473.07 cr (+18% YoY)** — all-time high
- **FY26 consolidated PAT INR 173.69 cr (+24% YoY)** — all-time high (33% PAT margin)
- **FY26 standalone PAT INR 174.39 cr** (+18% YoY, 39% PAT margin — one of the highest in listed India)
- **Business segments (FY26)**:
  - **Ratings** (core): INR 423.05 cr (+17%)
  - **Non-Ratings** (advisory + analytics + ESG + AI): crossed INR 50 cr (+19%)
- **International subsidiaries**:
  - **CareEdge Africa**: 100+ rated clients, US$4B debt rated (market leader)
  - **CareEdge Nepal**: Highest market share by instruments rated
  - **CareEdge Global IFSC**: First full year of operations
- **Turnaround completed** — CAAPL achieved breakeven FY26
- **AI platforms live**: EdgeAvira.AI, CredEdge, IntelEdge, Kalypto
- **Total dividend FY26: INR 22/share** (220% of face value) — INR 14 final + INR 8 interim

**Big picture in one sentence:** CARE Ratings is India's **#2 credit rating agency + emerging diversified financial services platform** — trading at **28.9x P/E on 26.3% ROCE + 1.34% dividend yield** with all-time-high PAT, expanding international presence (Africa + Nepal + IFSC), successful turnaround of loss-making subsidiaries, and emerging AI credit infrastructure. **The most attractive value + quality name in Indian financial services professional-owned space.**

## 2. Why This Industry Exists

Credit rating + adjacent services ride four durable forces:

**Force 1 — India's credit market growth.** Bank credit growing 16.1% in FY26 (up from 11% FY25). Corporate bond issuances softer but recovering. Every new bond + loan = potential rating revenue.

**Force 2 — Regulatory mandate.** SEBI + RBI + IRDAI all require third-party credit ratings for various instruments. This creates a regulatory moat that new entrants cannot bypass.

**Force 3 — ESG + climate risk expansion.** New rating categories (ESG, green bonds, climate risk) create new revenue streams.

**Force 4 — Non-ratings diversification (advisory + analytics + AI).** CARE building AI-credit platforms + industry research + wealth management insights.

**Where CARE sits.** #2 Indian credit rating agency. Duopoly-plus structure:
- **CRISIL** (S&P subsidiary) — #1, larger, MNC parent
- **CARE Ratings** — #2, Indian professional-owned
- **ICRA** (Moody's subsidiary) — #3, MNC-owned
- **India Ratings** (Fitch subsidiary) — #4, MNC-owned
- **Acuité, Brickwork** — smaller
- **CARE is the only large listed non-MNC-controlled** — creates unique investor positioning.
