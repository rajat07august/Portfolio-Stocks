## 2. Why This Industry Exists

Diversified financial services rides five durable forces:

**Force 1 — Indian equity market participation growth.** Demat accounts grew from 4 crore (Mar 2020) to 20+ crore (FY26). Multi-year retail participation.

**Force 2 — Wealth management shift.** HNI wealth in India growing 12%+ CAGR. PMS + AIF market expanding rapidly.

**Force 3 — IPO market activity.** India IPO market has been robust; Monarch is active in mid-cap IPO advisory.

**Force 4 — Mutual Fund industry AUM INR 60 lakh cr+.** Every new AMC has potential to capture share.

**Force 5 — Pre-IPO investing.** Emerging category as unicorns delay public listings.

**Where Monarch sits.** Mid-cap diversified financial services. Peers: Motilal Oswal (larger), Nuvama, IIFL Wealth, Anand Rathi (private), 5paisa. Monarch positioning: **rapid PAT compounding + expanding into higher-margin adjacent verticals**.
