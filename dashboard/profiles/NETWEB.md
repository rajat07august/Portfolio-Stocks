## 1. Business in Plain English

When India's Ministry of Electronics wants to build a sovereign AI cluster — thousands of GPUs stacked together to train Indian foundation models — they need someone to design, assemble, and deploy the whole computer stack: racks, cooling, storage, networking, software. **Netweb Technologies is India's only full-stack domestic provider of high-end computing systems** — supercomputers, AI compute clusters, private cloud stacks, and high-performance computing (HPC) infrastructure.

Think of it as **India's home-grown alternative to Dell/HPE/Nvidia for enterprise + government compute infrastructure**. Global peers cost 30–40% more when imported; Netweb makes them locally under "Make in India" and is qualified for sovereign compute programs.

The 30-second business snapshot:
- **HQ Faridabad, Haryana** — founded 1999 by the Lodha family (Sanjay Lodha, CMD; Navin Lodha, Whole-Time Director)
- **FY26 revenue INR 2,184 cr** (+90% YoY from INR 1,148 cr) — one of the fastest-growing India tech names
- **FY26 PAT INR 205.8 cr** (+81% YoY, 9.3% margin)
- **Three core pillars**: HPC (High-Performance Computing), Private Cloud, **AI Systems** (43.4% of FY26 revenue!)
- **AI Systems revenue grew 459.6% YoY in FY26** — the transformational shift
- **Order book INR 2,100 cr firm + INR 300 cr L1** = INR 2,400 cr entering FY27 (more than FY26 revenue on day 1)
- **India AI Mission alignment** — sovereign compute infrastructure narrative

**Big picture in one sentence:** Netweb is a **pure-play beneficiary of India's sovereign AI + AI Mission + Make in India** trifecta, with AI systems revenue growing 5x in FY26 and INR 2,400 cr order book on day 1 of FY27. Trades at premium multiples that price in continued exceptional growth — this is a growth-narrative name, not a value name.

## 2. Why This Industry Exists

Global compute infrastructure demand rides four durable forces:

**Force 1 — AI compute megatrend.** Global AI compute market is projected to grow from ~$60B (2024) to ~$500B (2030). Every enterprise + government needs AI compute clusters for training + inference of foundation models. Nvidia's shortage + hyperscaler dependency creates opportunity for local players.

**Force 2 — Sovereign AI infrastructure.** Every major country is investing in "sovereign AI" — compute + data + models built + owned within the country. India's IndiaAI Mission ($1.2B+ initial funding, targeting INR 10,000+ cr over 5 years) is directly focused on domestic compute infrastructure.

**Force 3 — Make in India Mandate.** Government + PSU + defence + banking customers get preferential pricing for domestic manufacturers. Netweb is the only Indian full-stack HPC provider — massive first-mover advantage.

**Force 4 — Data centre + cloud expansion.** India's data centre capacity to grow from ~1 GW (2024) to ~5 GW (2030). Every data centre needs racks, networking, cooling, and compute infrastructure.

**Where Netweb sits in the map.** It's a **standalone niche player** — no direct listed competitor in India:
- **Dell / HPE / Cisco** — imported alternatives, more expensive, no local mfg
- **Yotta Data Services** — data centre operator, not compute provider
- **Palo Alto Networks / Zscaler** — security, adjacent
- **Netweb is uniquely positioned** as the *only* full-stack Indian provider

## 3. What Makes This Company Different

**Moat 1 — Only full-stack Indian HPC/AI provider.** Design, assembly, deployment, software — all done in-house in Faridabad. Cannot be replicated quickly.

**Moat 2 — Sovereign compute qualification.** Approved by MeitY, IndiaAI, defence, PSU customers for critical infrastructure. Certification takes 3–5 years.

**Moat 3 — AI Systems (43.4% of FY26 revenue, growing 459% YoY).** This is the transformational bet — Netweb has cracked GPU cluster design + power + cooling for AI workloads. Global AI compute grow rate is faster than Netweb can produce.

**Moat 4 — INR 2,400 cr order book entering FY27.** More than FY26 revenue on day 1. Multi-year visibility.

**Moat 5 — 27-year vintage + Lodha family relationships.** Sanjay Lodha founded 1999. Deep relationships across BSNL, MTNL, ISRO, DRDO, banks, government.

**Moat 6 — R&D investment.** Netweb designs GPU cluster racks + water cooling + BMS software in-house. IP creation vs pure hardware assembly.

**Moat 7 — Government + PSU customer base.** Sticky, multi-year contracts, less price-sensitive than commercial customers.

**Moat 8 — HPC/Private Cloud steady base.** Even without AI, HPC + Private Cloud grew mid-teens% — a stable base.

**Where the moat is weaker.**
- Nvidia dependency for GPUs — supply constraint (though Netweb is a preferred customer).
- Retail-heavy shareholder base (243,000 shareholders, up from 75k in 2 years).
- Customer concentration (top customers = large government projects).
- Currency exposure (GPU imports).
