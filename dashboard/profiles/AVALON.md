**⚠️ Read the two columns against each other.** The +49.8% revenue and +145.3% PAT are real, but they are measured against **Q1FY26, which was the weakest quarter of the last eight** (₹323cr revenue, ₹14cr PAT). Against the immediately preceding quarter, **revenue rose 0.9% and PAT fell 15.3%.**

The deck describes this as the *"eighth consecutive quarter of sequential"* growth. On revenue that is technically true — but at **+0.9% it is the thinnest of the eight**, and profit went backwards. **My read: the YoY comparison flatters, the QoQ comparison is the honest run-rate signal, and the truth is that Avalon has plateaued around the ₹480cr quarterly mark for two quarters.** Not a break in the thesis; a pause worth naming.

**What genuinely improved:**
- **Order book ₹2,208cr as of 30-Jun-2026, +23.4% YoY**, executable over an average 14 months. Long-term contracts ₹1,256cr.
- **Net working capital 142 days (Jun-25) → 117 days (Jun-26)** — a 25-day improvement, and the single best operational number in the print. Management had guided 120–130 days; they are through it.
- **India manufacturing delivered 16.7% EBITDA and 11.1% PAT margins in Q1** — the domestic operation is already running at healthy profitability.
- **🆕 Box-build reached 60% of revenue for the first time** (from ~54%). Box-build is full system integration rather than bare PCB assembly, and it carries higher margin — this is the mix shift the whole margin thesis depends on.

### 🌍 The India/US structure, clarified

The v1 profile called this a "hybrid India+US model" without quantifying it. From the Q4FY26 call, the two figures that matter and are easily conflated:

- **62% of FY26 revenue came from the US market; only 23% was manufactured in the US.** The gap is US customers served out of Indian plants.
- **India business grew 29% YoY; US business grew 59% YoY.**
- Management's framing of why both matter: *"Our presence in both India and U.S. gives customers the option to start in the U.S. and later transition to India or come directly to India."*

**That optionality is the actual moat** — it is a customer-acquisition funnel, not just a manufacturing footprint. It is also why US manufacturing can run at a loss and still be strategically justified.

## 1. Business in Plain English

Avalon Technologies is India's **most US-focused EMS company** — it earns 62% of its revenue from US market customers (either through export from India or through its 15 US manufacturing facilities). Where Syrma is a broad domestic EMS play and Aimtron is a niche India-first ESDM player, **Avalon is the hybrid India+US model** — same manufacturing capability positioned in two geographies to serve global customers with dual sourcing.

The 30-second business snapshot:
- **HQ Chennai, Tamil Nadu** — but 15 manufacturing units across US and India
- Founded/led by **Kunhamed Bicha** (Chairman & MD)
- **FY26 revenue INR 1,603 cr** (+46% YoY from INR 1,098 cr)
- **FY26 EBITDA INR 173 cr (10.8% margin, +51% YoY)**
- **FY26 PAT INR 113 cr (+78% YoY, 6.9% margin)**
- **Six manufacturing verticals**: Semiconductor equipment, Aerospace/Defence, Industrial, Medical devices, Clean tech, Communications
- **Box build revenue 54%** (up from 49% in FY25) — critical margin lever
- **Order book INR 2,196 cr** (executable over 14 months) + INR 1,245 cr long-term contracts (14–36 months)
- India 79% of manufacturing revenue; US 21%; **US market customers = 62% of total revenue**

**Big picture in one sentence:** Avalon is the **India-US hybrid EMS platform** riding US tariff normalisation (50% → 18%) + India-Europe trade deal + semiconductor + aerospace megatrends, with 7 consecutive quarters of sequential improvement in FY26. Priced at 107x P/E — expensive, but the box-build shift + hybrid manufacturing model is genuinely differentiated.

## 2. Why This Industry Exists

Global EMS + Indian manufacturing rides four durable forces (already elaborated in Syrma profile — same context applies):

1. Electronics demand acceleration
2. PLI schemes accelerating
3. China +1 supply chain rebalancing (Avalon uniquely benefits — has both India + US mfg)
4. Semiconductor + defence + medical megatrends

**Avalon's unique differentiator: India + US hybrid model.** Same customer, dual sourcing option. Post-Trump tariff era, this is valuable — US customers want US-mfg option for critical products.

## 3. What Makes This Company Different

**Moat 1 — Hybrid India + US manufacturing (15 units).** Only listed Indian EMS with material US manufacturing footprint. Cannot be replicated quickly.

**Moat 2 — 54% box build revenue (up from 49% YoY).** Higher-value / higher-margin than pure PCBA. Box build capability = OEM stickiness.

**Moat 3 — Order book INR 2,196 cr (+25% YoY) + INR 1,245 cr long-term contracts.** ~2 years of executable book.

**Moat 4 — Six-vertical diversification.** Semiconductor + Aerospace + Industrial + Medical + Clean tech + Communications. No single vertical dominates.

**Moat 5 — Semiconductor equipment breakthrough.** Aligns with India Semiconductor Mission 2.0. New program wins in Q4FY26.

**Moat 6 — Capex-light hybrid model.** Asset turns 9.9x (up from 7.5x FY25). ROCE 19.5%.

**Moat 7 — 7 consecutive quarters of sequential improvement.** Consistency signals disciplined execution.

**Moat 8 — Tariff arbitrage.** During 50% US tariff period, Avalon recovered 99% of tariffs from customers due to long-standing relationships. Now tariffs reduced to 18%.

**Where the moat is weaker.**
- Retail-heavy shareholder base (65,594 shareholders).
- Promoter holding dropped from 51.24% to 44.41% — significant dilution.
- Capex-light means limited long-cycle differentiation vs pure India EMS players.
- US labour cost higher than India — margin structure varies by geography.
