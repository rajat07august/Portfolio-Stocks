## 1. Business in Plain English

Sunita Tools is a **small-cap SME precision machining company** — CNC machining, grinding, mould base manufacturing, and steel plate machining — with facilities in **Vasai (Maharashtra)**, **Ahmedabad**, and **Coimbatore** (recently acquired). Younger, smaller, and more entrepreneurial than the mid-large auto components players, targeting the precision engineering + aerospace + mould-based industries.

The 30-second business snapshot:
- **HQ Vasai, Maharashtra** — plus recently acquired Ahmedabad (Sunita Leo) + Coimbatore (Sunita Imperial) units
- **First-time promoter:** *Mr. Satish Kumar Pandey* — Managing Director AND CFO (both roles on one person = classic SME structure)
- **Small revenue base** (INR ~50-70 cr range based on H1FY25 data)
- **H1FY25 volume growth 38-40%**; revenue slightly lower due to 45% steel price drop
- **Long-term debt-free** (paid off last loan Aug 2024)
- **Recent acquisitions**: Sunita Leo (Ahmedabad), Sunita Imperial (Coimbatore in progress), LOI with Chennai mould base company
- **Aerospace orders** at Vasai factory 2
- **Distribution partnerships**: Saudi + UAE (early stage)
- **Screener listing: Sunita Tools Ltd — CMP ₹741, MCap INR 465 cr, P/E 73.5x**

**Big picture in one sentence:** Sunita Tools is a **founder-driven SME precision machining player** on an acquisition-led growth strategy — Ahmedabad + Coimbatore + Chennai expansions — but with meaningful promoter dilution (72.98% → 62.75% over 2 years) and only 1,733 shareholders (extreme illiquidity). Priced at 73.5x P/E on modest returns (ROCE 10.1%, ROE 10.8%) — **the most speculative name in the auto components sector**.
