### 🔴 The finding: half of FY26's record profit landed in one quarter, and it was largely non-operating

FY26 was genuinely a record year on the headline. It is much less impressive on inspection.

**Quarterly PAT through FY26 (consolidated, ₹cr):** Jun-25 **65** → Sep-25 **83** → **Dec-25 347** → Mar-26 **196**. Full year ₹692cr.

**The December quarter alone was ₹347cr — 50.1% of the entire year's consolidated profit**, on OPM of 29% against 8%, 13% and 16% in the other three quarters. Management explained it across two calls, and the explanation is threefold — none of it recurring:

1. **A liquidated-damages provision reversal on a single project.** Q3FY26 call, an analyst asked whether the LD reversal covered one project or several. Management: *"**This figure is for one project only.**"* On the mechanism: *"whenever schedule time is gone, we start keeping provision for the LD. And whenever project is completed, we apply for the time extension… most of the time, we get the time extension and we reverse the provision. This is the routine practice of EIL."* Routine practice, yes — but a reversal of this magnitude is not routine, and it is booked in *provision for guarantee and warranty*, not in operations.
2. **A change order above ₹200cr.** Q4FY26 call, verbatim: *"in the last quarter there was some chain [change] orders that we received from the client and there was a chain order of **more than Rs.200 crores**, which we considered in the previous quarter. **And that is why in the previous quarter, profits were so high.**"*
3. **₹24cr dividend from Numaligarh Refinery (NRL)**, recognised in Q3.

**Screener flags the aggregate independently as a con: *"Earnings include an other income of Rs.242 Cr"*** — against consolidated PAT of ₹692cr, that is **35% of profit from non-operating sources.**

**⚠️ What this does to the valuation, which is the whole point.** If Q3FY26 had printed in line with Q4's ₹196cr rather than ₹347cr, FY26 PAT would be roughly **₹540cr, not ₹692cr**. On that basis:

| | Reported | Normalized |
|---|---|---|
| FY26 PAT | ₹692 cr | **~₹495–540 cr** |
| **P/E at ₹12,520cr market cap** | **18.1x** | **~23–25x** |

**EIL is not an 18x stock. It is roughly a 24x stock that had a good quarter of provision reversals.** That single adjustment substantially weakens the v1 thesis, which rested on *"19.3x P/E on 30.6% ROCE"* as a value-plus-quality anchor. The quality is real; the value is mostly optical.

## 1. Business in Plain English

Engineers India Limited (EIL) is India's **premier public-sector engineering consultancy** — specialising in **oil refineries, petrochemicals, fertiliser plants, and process industries**. When Indian Oil Corporation, ONGC, or Reliance Petroleum builds a new refinery, they typically engage EIL for **process design + detailed engineering + procurement management + construction supervision** (the "consultancy" business) OR for **turnkey EPC delivery** (the "turnkey" segment). Founded 1965 as a joint Indo-French venture, EIL is now a **Government of India Maharatna-status engineering consultancy** — 51.32% government-owned + globally recognised in refinery engineering.

The 30-second business snapshot:
- **HQ New Delhi** — founded 1965 (60-year heritage)
- **Government of India holding**: 51.32% — completely stable
- **FY26 revenue INR 3,849 cr (+27% YoY)** — highest ever
- **FY26 PAT INR 638 cr (+37% YoY)** — highest ever (standalone)
- **FY26 EBITDA INR 877 cr (+33%)** — margin 21.6% (up from 20.6%)
- **Order book: INR 15,109 cr — all-time high** (up from INR 11,717 cr)
- **Order inflow FY26: INR 7,979 cr**
- **Two segments** (FY26 revenue mix):
  - **Consultancy & Engineering: INR 1,782 cr** (higher-margin)
  - **Turnkey: INR 2,067 cr**
- **Subsidiary CEIL PAT: INR 24.71 cr** (+20% YoY)
- **100% dividend paid** (INR 5 per share on face value INR 5)

**Big picture in one sentence:** EIL is India's **flagship engineering consultancy** in oil & gas, delivering record FY26 numbers (revenue +27%, PAT +37%) with all-time-high order book (INR 15,109 cr) — trading at **19.3x P/E on 30.6% ROCE + 1.68% dividend yield**. **Excellent value + quality combination in the EPC space.**

## 2. Why This Industry Exists

Oil & gas EPC + consultancy rides four durable forces:

**Force 1 — India's oil refining capex.** India's oil refining capacity is being expanded from 250 MMTPA (FY24) to 400+ MMTPA by 2030. Every refinery expansion = INR 5,000-15,000 cr consultancy + EPC opportunity.

**Force 2 — Petrochemicals + fertiliser buildout.** Major PSU + private capex — HPCL, IOCL, BPCL, GAIL, RIL, Chambal, IFFCO. Multi-year visibility.

**Force 3 — International expansion.** EIL exports consultancy to Middle East (challenges due to conflict), Africa (growth market), and Southeast Asia.

**Force 4 — Non-oil diversification.** Metallurgy, water infrastructure, defence — EIL expanding capability portfolio.

**Where EIL sits.** #1 Indian process engineering consultancy. Domestic peers: private consultancies (TCE, Tata Projects). International peers: Bechtel, Fluor, KBR, Wood plc. EIL's positioning: dominant PSU-relationship base + technical depth + global project experience.
