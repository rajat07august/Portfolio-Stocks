## 1. Business in Plain English

When a farmer in Punjab, UP, or Maharashtra decides to spray a chemical to kill weeds, pests, or fungal disease on his crop, he goes to a local agri-dealer and asks for a specific brand — Torry Super, Centran, Amuse, Altair, Sparcle. **Insecticides India (IIL) is India's largest domestic-focused agrochemical formulations + manufacturing company** — synthesises the active ingredients, formulates them into ready-to-spray products, distributes via 65,000+ dealers, and increasingly manufactures technical grade actives in-house for import substitution.

The 30-second business snapshot:
- **HQ Delhi (Registered) + Multiple manufacturing plants in Rajasthan (Chopanki), Gujarat (Dahej), Uttarakhand** — founded 1996 by Rajesh Kumar Aggarwal (still MD, 30-year founder tenure)
- **Aggarwal family** running as founder-promoters — small family group
- **FY26 revenue growth 7% (profit neutral); Q4FY26 growth 19%; Q4 premium products +24%**
- **25+ new product launches over last 3 years** (premiumisation strategy)
- **Global partnerships**: Nissan Chemical (Japan) — Altair; Corteva (US) — Sparcle, Granuvia, upcoming Spinoace + Green Mix
- **R&D in-house**: Torry Super, Centran SC, Centran granule, Amuse — indigenous R&D success
- **Two segments**: B2C (retail brand agrochemicals) + B2B (technical grade actives for other formulators)

**Big picture in one sentence:** Insecticides India is India's **largest independent domestic-focused agrochemical formulator** with an in-house R&D + global partnership strategy — trading at 13.8x P/E on 15.9% ROCE with rock-stable 72.30% promoter holding across 12 quarters (zero dilution) and Q4FY26 accelerating to 19% growth. **Deeply undervalued relative to sector peers**.

## 2. Why This Industry Exists

Indian agrochemicals rides five durable forces:

**Force 1 — India's crop protection demand.** India's crop protection market is INR 24,000+ cr (FY24), growing 8-10% CAGR. Multi-decade tailwind driven by rising yields + climate stress + government minimum support prices.

**Force 2 — Premiumisation.** Legacy chemicals losing share to new-generation actives with better safety + efficacy. Premium products carry 30-40% higher realizations.

**Force 3 — Import substitution.** India historically imports 30%+ of technical grade actives from China. Government policy + tariff structures + PLI-like incentives all pushing Indian formulators toward in-house technical manufacturing.

**Force 4 — Global partnerships model.** Global innovators (Nissan, Corteva, Syngenta, Bayer) increasingly partner with Indian distributors for market access — Indian formulators get exclusive product rights.

**Force 5 — Monsoon-linked cycle.** FY26 India had good monsoon → strong FY26 second-half growth. FY27 similar expectations.

**Where IIL sits.** Largest independent Indian agrochemical formulator (not a JV with a global). Peers: PI Industries (larger, export-heavy), Dhanuka Agritech (comparable size), Bharat Rasayan (contract mfg focused), Astec Lifesciences (technical grade focused).
