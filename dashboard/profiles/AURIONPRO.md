## 1. Business in Plain English

Aurionpro Solutions is a **diversified enterprise technology company** — not a single-vertical IT services firm — with 4 distinct business lines: **Banking software** (core banking + AI-native trade finance + workflow automation), **Transit technology** (traffic management, tolling, smart mobility), **Data Center infrastructure** (hyperscaler + AI-focused deployments), and **AI software** (Aurion AI native platforms, foundation models for banking). The company is going through a strategic transformation from a traditional BFSI software vendor into an **AI-native enterprise technology platform** — with FY26 marking heavy investment in Software 2.0 (agentic architecture) products.

The 30-second business snapshot:
- **HQ Mumbai** — Group CEO & Vice-Chairman: Ashish Rai (execution + AI strategy lead)
- **CFO**: Vipul Parmar
- **FY26 revenue INR 1,411 cr (+20.2% YoY)** — missed 30% growth plan (planned pre-Middle East)
- **FY26 EBITDA margin 20.02%** (within guided 20-21% band, lower end)
- **FY26 PAT margin 15.02%** (within guided 15-16% band, lower end)
- **Order book: INR 1,800+ cr** entering FY27
- **Four business verticals**:
  - **Banking** (core banking + Aurion AI native trade finance platform launched)
  - **Transit** (India strong, going global)
  - **Data Center** (won largest Indian AI-focused data center mandate from hyperscaler)
  - **AI Software** (Arya + Lexsi Labs foundation models tuned for banking)
- **Software 2.0 build-out**: Entire Aurionpro stack being rebuilt for agentic architecture over 2 quarters
- **Vision 2030** long-term positioning

**Big picture in one sentence:** Aurionpro is a **diversified BFSI + Transit + Data Center + AI enterprise tech firm** in a **strategic transformation year** — FY26 delivered 20.2% growth (below 30% plan due to Middle East war impact + hyperscaler execution delay), but heavy AI Software 2.0 investment underway with order book INR 1,800+ cr entering FY27. **High-conviction transformation play at 22.3x P/E with clear FY27 catalyst.**

## 2. Why This Industry Exists

Aurionpro plays across 4 verticals — each with distinct forces:

**Force 1 — Banking AI transformation.** Global banks moving from traditional workflow software to agentic AI systems. Trade finance + KYC + AML + treasury — all being reimagined for AI-native architecture.

**Force 2 — India + global transit tech.** Traffic management + tolling + smart mobility becoming mission-critical for smart cities.

**Force 3 — AI infrastructure buildout (data centers).** Hyperscalers (Google, AWS, Microsoft) + India-domestic AI compute all needing data center design + engineering.

**Force 4 — Middle East BFSI capex (cyclically challenging).** Middle East banks were a growth engine for Aurionpro; currently disrupted by geopolitics.

**Where Aurionpro sits.** Mid-cap diversified enterprise tech. Peers: Persistent Systems (larger, similar diversified), Coforge, LTIMindtree, Tata Elxsi, Cyient (adjacent). Aurionpro's differentiator: **four-vertical spread + AI Software 2.0 native build**.
