## 1. Business in Plain English

Every large factory, warehouse, commercial building, or industrial customer in India that wants to reduce electricity bills has three options: (a) install **rooftop solar** with capex, (b) sign a **captive open-access solar PPA** where a developer builds a solar plant elsewhere and wheels power via grid, or (c) install **BESS (Battery Energy Storage)** for time-shift arbitrage. **Oriana Power builds all three** — it's a **renewable energy EPC + developer + operator** targeting commercial + industrial (C&I) customers across India, plus utility-scale solar tenders.

## 2. Why This Industry Exists

Renewable EPC + C&I open-access rides five durable forces:

**Force 1 — India's 500 GW renewable target by 2030.** Government mandate + INR 10 lakh cr+ multi-year capex.

**Force 2 — C&I customer economics.** Commercial customers save 30-40% on power bill with solar PPA. Multi-year contracted revenue for developers.

**Force 3 — BESS mandate + tenders.** RE integration requires BESS. Government tenders scaling rapidly.

**Force 4 — Open Access + Captive policy tailwind.** Regulatory framework enabling private developers to sell power directly to C&I.

**Force 5 — Rooftop residential + institutional.** PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana + state schemes creating rooftop demand.

**Where Oriana sits.** Fast-growing mid-cap renewable EPC + developer. Peers: **Suzlon Energy** (much larger, wind-focused), **Waaree Renewables** (module + EPC), **Sterling & Wilson Renewable Energy** (larger EPC), **Adani Green** (utility-scale developer), **KP Green Engineering**. Oriana positioning: **C&I open-access + captive solar EPC focused with rapid scale-up**.
