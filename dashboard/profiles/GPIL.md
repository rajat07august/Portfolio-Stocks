## 1. Business in Plain English

Godawari Power & Ispat (GPIL) is a **fully vertically-integrated iron & steel maker in Chhattisgarh** — mining its own iron ore, making pellets from ore, converting pellets to sponge iron, then to billets, then to structural rolled products (rebars, TMT bars, angles) — plus captive power generation and ferroalloys. Where most Indian steel makers buy inputs and add value at one stage, **GPIL owns the entire value chain from mine to end product**.

The 30-second business snapshot:
- **HQ Raipur, Chhattisgarh** (Siltara Industrial Area) — Hira Group (Agrawal family)
- **Executive Directors**: Abhishek Agrawal (2nd generation), Dinesh Gandhi
- **CFO**: Sanjay Bothra
- **FY26 revenue steady** (softer realization YoY offsets volume growth)
- **FY26 EBITDA INR 1,253 cr (23% margin — strong for steel)**
- **FY26 PAT INR 802 cr (15% margin — high for the sector)**
- **Q4FY26 EBITDA INR 439 cr (+38% YoY, +91% QoQ)**
- **Q4FY26 PAT INR 280 cr; Q4 revenue +41% QoQ** (major momentum turn)
- **Business verticals**: Iron ore mining, pellets, sponge iron, billets, structural rolled products, ferroalloys, captive power (~140 MW+)

**Big picture in one sentence:** GPIL is India's **most vertically-integrated mid-cap steel maker** — mining + pellets + sponge + billets + structural + ferroalloys + captive power — with FY26 delivering 23% EBITDA margin (best-in-class), Q4 momentum turning up dramatically (+41% QoQ revenue, +91% QoQ EBITDA), and 20.4% ROCE at 21x P/E. **Momentum turn story with quality metrics.**

## 2. Why This Industry Exists

Indian steel + iron ore rides four durable forces:

**Force 1 — India's steel consumption growth.** India steel demand growing 8-10% CAGR through 2030, driven by infrastructure + construction + auto + capital goods. India will be world's 2nd largest steel consumer.

**Force 2 — Iron ore price cycle.** Steel pricing cycles with iron ore + coking coal. Vertical integration (GPIL owns iron ore mine) = margin protection.

**Force 3 — Structural steel demand (rebars + TMT).** Infrastructure + housing + real estate drive rebar demand.

**Force 4 — Regional advantage (Chhattisgarh).** Raw material proximity, transport savings, energy availability.

**Where GPIL sits.** Mid-cap integrated player. Peers: Sarda Energy & Minerals (comparable size), Jai Balaji Industries, Shyam Metalics, Jindal Steel & Power, Tata Steel, JSW Steel (much larger).
