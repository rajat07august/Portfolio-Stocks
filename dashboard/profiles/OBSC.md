## 1. Business in Plain English

Where Happy Forgings + Sansera do precision forgings + machining, **OBSC Perfection is a multi-process precision manufacturer** — CNC machining + turning + grinding + specialty coatings + heat treatment + assembly — for automotive + non-automotive customers. Younger + smaller than the big players but growing very fast, and deliberately shifting from single-process (CNC only) to multi-process capability.

The 30-second business snapshot:
- **HQ Delhi/NCR (Jasola)** — founded ~2017 by the Leekha family (Saksham Leekha, MD)
- **FY26 revenue growth: 54%** (from smaller base)
- **FY26 EBITDA margin 19.5%**
- **Exports: 20% of revenue** (up +50%), serving 17 countries
- **Non-automotive revenue +150%**
- **Order book INR 1,200+ cr** — very strong coverage
  - Auto order book: INR 980 cr
  - Non-auto order book: INR 230 cr
  - Execution: 6–7 years
- **Preferential issue INR 43.3 cr** (FY26)
- **Building "mega factories"** — capex phase

**Big picture in one sentence:** OBSC is a **young, fast-growing precision components company** transitioning from single-process CNC to multi-process capability, with 54% FY26 growth + INR 1,200 cr order book on 6–7 year execution horizon. Founder-family driven, in aggressive capex phase, priced accordingly.
