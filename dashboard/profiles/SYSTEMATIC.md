## 1. Business in Plain English

Every high-voltage electricity transmission line in India — the tall lattice steel towers running from power stations to substations — requires **specialty steel wires** to carry the current + **OPGW (Optical Ground Wire)** running on top. OPGW is the modern innovation — it combines two functions in one cable: (a) **protective ground wire** for lightning strikes (essential for transmission tower safety) and (b) **fiber optic strands inside** carrying data at gigabit speed for grid communication + telecom backhaul. **Systematic Industries is a Mumbai-based specialty steel wire + OPGW manufacturer** serving power transmission utilities (state DISCOMs + national transmission players) with the tagline "Powering India's Grid and Digital Backbone."

## 2. Why This Industry Exists

Steel wires + OPGW rides five durable forces:

**Force 1 — India Grid Modernization (Viksit Bharat 2047).** Draft National Electricity Policy 2026 mandates transmission expansion + grid strengthening. Every new transmission tower needs steel wires.

**Force 2 — OPGW dual-function economics.** Combines ground protection + fiber optic communication. Every new transmission line increasingly deploys OPGW instead of separate ground wire + telecom cable.

**Force 3 — Renewable Energy integration mandates.** RE parks (2,000+ GW target) need transmission infrastructure = steel wire + OPGW demand.

**Force 4 — Digital backbone expansion.** BharatNet + telecom fiber-to-tower requires OPGW for grid + telecom integration.

**Force 5 — Import substitution.** BIS licenses + regulatory framework favor Indian manufacturers over imports.

**Where Systematic sits.** Small-cap Indian specialty steel wire producer with OPGW expansion. Peers: **Sterlite Technologies** (larger, OPGW + fiber), **KEC International** (transmission tower EPC), **Skipper** (transmission towers), **Bharat Wire Ropes** (steel wire ropes). Systematic positioning: **niche steel wire + emerging OPGW combined with 20+ BIS licenses**.
